Modifications in UmadLab:
  - Renamed plugin identity to UmadLab (assembly, manifest, window title);
    namespaces remain AnoMech.*
  - Added full P1-P5 scenarios under Scenarios/Umad/ (P1Full .. P5Full) with
    CN party-finder AI strats
  - Added the P1-P5 encounter chain controller (UmadEncounterChain): waymark
    presets, configurable countdown, cheat-sheet toggles, per-stage checkpoints
  - Scenario menu reduced to one "Full" scenario per phase; the specialised
    scenarios keep their sources but are no longer registered
  - Assorted mechanic fixes (P3 black hole tethers, P4 Kefka Says cheat sheet,
    Samurai Fuka GCD handling)
